# The Stillwater Hotel — The Lower Register

A Backrooms-inspired interactive horror mystery in English and Simplified Chinese, hidden inside an unassuming hotel website. The public site retains its ivory paper, bronze rules, traditional typography, and deep-teal masthead. An apparently successful checkout exposes a second, corrupted website and a longer investigation below the hotel.

This edition includes both story chapters, twelve records, and linked puzzles. Public copy, guest accounts, and reports use different voices; documents have a Back button that restores inputs and selections. Completed stations open their saved record with an explicit replay option.

## New recruitment opening

First-time visitors start at **Shiftwork**, a fictional hospitality jobs website. The player has just left a hotel reception job and is looking for work with accommodation. Search, category filters, vacancy details and applications are interactive. Every advertised vacancy rejects the application. After all six jobs have been attempted, a message from the Stillwater Hotel appears in the bottom-right corner. Following it opens the original hotel site with an applicant notice explaining the temporary room and reception registration.

Applications stay in the browser and are never submitted to employers. No real CV, account or contact details are needed. Partial application attempts survive reloads and language switches. Returning hotel players keep their save and bypass the introduction; **Replay the job search** in the hotel footer replays the opening without clearing hotel progress.

The hotel layout, two chapters, twelve records, original puzzle answers and five endings remain. Chinese text uses direct hotel service language on public pages and conversational narrative in the mystery. Lower puzzles have three optional hint levels, selection counts and undo controls; the radio accepts exact numeric entry as well as the slider.

## Download and play

On the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/RieyekiWong/123), choose **Code → Download ZIP**. Extract the entire archive and open the root `index.html` with Chrome or Edge. Do not open the HTML from inside the compressed archive. Extract this edition into a new folder rather than mixing its scripts with an old download.

All images, scripts, and styles are included. Five real hotel photographs replace the earlier generated hotel scenes. Photographer names, original source pages, and licenses are available through **Photo credits**, `assets/CREDITS.md`, and `assets/photo-credits.json`. These photos illustrate a fictional setting; their real locations and staff are not involved in the story. The English entry is `index.html`; the Simplified Chinese entry is `index-zh.html`. Header links switch editions and retain the current page. Both use the same progress schema and share saved progress when served from the same origin. Local-file storage isolation is browser-dependent.

There are no runtime packages, remote fonts, external image services, sign-ups, API keys, or backend services. If your browser or organization blocks local HTML, use the local development server below.

## Support conversations

Contact supervisor and Contact support open a local messaging interface with conversation bubbles, keyword suggestions above the composer, and editable free-text messages. Suggestions fill the composer; the player still sends the message. Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a line, with IME composition protected.

Normally, the first thread to reach its third sent message briefly loses its connection. Three consecutive time questions instead summon the impostor first; after acknowledging it, a further message allows the original interruption. Dylan’s plea arrives in stages for **15 seconds**, then the normal conversation returns without keeping the injected messages in its transcript. The deadline persists across closing, reloading and language changes and is triggered once per playthrough. Reduced-motion and quiet-effects settings keep the text readable without the stronger visual distortion.

The public homepage contains ordinary support and recruitment contacts, not Dylan’s plea. A guest review signed **D. H.** has an attached message that reveals the trail and preserves the camera-order clue if the temporary interruption was missed. Existing saves retain both story branches, surveillance checks and the original lower investigation. Support messages and drafts are stored locally and never sent to a service or model API.

## Two chapters

**The public hotel** contains rooms, a history, guest messages, fictional registration, the opening-night register, security footage, and a night-desk archive. The Hotel Guide adds dining and radio information, a reading room, courtyard walks, service instructions, residents' records, and lost property. The expanded employee archive includes an incident chronology and an unresolved night audit.

**The lower register** begins when the completed public investigation reaches the service stair. The apparent group checkout is rejected. The entire site switches to a dark, red-toned presentation, and an unsettling document interrupts the departure. Public information remains available through the navigation; a persistent banner returns to the lower chapter.

Six linked stations require different kinds of reasoning:

1. Reconstruct a four-circuit service sequence.
2. Tune an FM receiver using a retained programme card.
3. Extract a word from a printed menu.
4. Restore an erased identity using original records.
5. Read a reversed message in the observation glass.
6. Put three incident events in chronological order.

A final discharge combines the recovered instructions. An alternative staff appointment is a false resolution. In total, five departure records can be remembered, including the true ending. Completed stations stay unlocked and can be reviewed without duplicating evidence.

All required clues are inside this website. No real-world contact or outside investigation is needed. Source links beside the lower puzzles let you revisit relevant documents, and the notebook gives contextual pointers to missing records. Use the document header’s **Back** button to return without losing a typed answer, selected circuit sequence, tuned frequency, or selected incident cards. Camera channel changes replace the current feed, so Back returns directly to the directory. Returning from guidelines to the current registration page retains unfinished form fields.

## Horror settings and local data

This edition includes three different generated creature portraits (night porter, veiled visitor, and distorted reflection) and short visual interruptions. Consecutive encounters rotate artwork rather than repeat the same face. Portraits are preloaded when the security recordings are opened, not on the ordinary homepage. **Site Information** in the footer controls sudden visual effects; turning them off hides the figures and interruptions without disabling any puzzle or the second chapter. Reduced-motion preferences suppress animated interruptions. There is no rapid flashing.

Ambient audio defaults to off. When explicitly enabled, Web Audio generates a quiet ventilation hum and short, low-volume knocks during some encounters. No audio is required to solve anything: the radio has a written transcript. Press Escape or use the close button to dismiss any document. The story never opens browser windows or requests fullscreen.

Nicknames, fictional registrations, twelve collected records, solved stations, preferences, five local guestbook notes, and remembered departures stay in this browser's `localStorage`. No note or reservation is sent to a server. Clearing site data erases progress. If storage is unavailable, play continues in the current tab.

Earlier progress from the first two editions is retained when possible. Players who already completed the public story can revisit Room 404 and choose the service stair to enter the new chapter without resetting. A reset in Site Information clears both chapters, closes audio, and retains the visual-effects preference. Chinese names and decoded mirror instructions are accepted alongside their English answers; the menu acrostic and reversed original remain bilingual so the puzzle logic is unchanged.

The Stillwater story, characters, correspondence, keypad, bookings, apparent site compromise, and error messages are fictional. The employee code is a story puzzle, not authentication. Hotel photographs are real and retain their individual Creative Commons licenses; horror portraits are generated fictional characters. See the bundled credits for exact provenance.

## Development

Requires Node.js 20 or newer; verified with Node.js 24.

```bash
npm ci
npm run dev
```

The server uses port `3000`. Select another port with:

```bash
npm run dev -- --port 8080
```

This is a plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript project. All application scripts are deferred classic scripts with no module imports so extracted copies can be played without installing dependencies. Reload after editing source files.

## Build and deployment

```bash
npm run build
```

Publish the **contents** of `dist/` to a static host such as GitHub Pages. Relative asset paths and hash navigation support repository subdirectories. Uploading source does not enable an online website automatically. No Node.js server is required for static hosting.

## Validation

```bash
npm run build
npm test
```

Service tests validate source and built resources, the expansion script, images, 404 handling, and protection of project metadata.

```bash
npx playwright install chromium
npm run test:e2e
```

The configuration uses `/usr/bin/chromium` when present, or `CHROMIUM_PATH` if specified. Browser tests start a separate service on port `3107` and cover:

- A full unseeded investigation through both chapters, all twelve records, all five departures, and reset.
- Wrong answers, station dependencies, adjustable frequency, sequence clearing, and discharge validation.
- Saved station progress, duplicate evidence prevention, and old-edition continuation.
- Printed source documents, English text, keyboard dismissal, preference persistence, reduced motion, and disabled visual effects.
- Document history, restored puzzle controllers and form fields, camera navigation, distinct scare artwork, and visible photograph credits.
- Registration, guestbook limits, escaped text, optional audio, corrupt or blocked storage, and browser navigation.
- Public and corrupted layouts at phone, tablet, and desktop widths.

### Public hotel

1. Register under a nickname to receive the Room 307 key.
2. Read the bedside card or Room 307 diary. Record the stopped time: **04:04**.
3. Open the floor directory, review the security recording, and save **CAM 02** showing Room 404.
4. Save **Rowan's warning** from the guestbook.
5. Copy the **opening-night register** in Our History. The hotel opened **03 October 1987**.
6. Enter the employee code **1003**: opening day, month first.
7. Record the **maintenance log** and **Eleanor's letter**. You now have six public records.
8. Unlock Room 404 with **0404**. The first two choices lead to Dylan’s rescue trail and the surveillance shift; see the side-story walkthrough above.
9. With a room key and all six public records, choose the service stair. The group checkout is rejected and the lower register opens.

### The lower register

1. **Switchboard:** use the facilities sheet. Reception, kitchen, linen, night desk = **3 → 1 → 4 → 2**. Clear a mistaken sequence before trying again.
2. **Receiver:** the dining-room programme lists **88.7 FM**. Tune the slider to 88.7 MHz and decode the transcript.
3. **Census cabinet:** read the first letters of the five dinner courses: Smoked, Thyme, Iced, Lemon, Lavender = **STILL**.
4. **Missing resident:** the original photograph and residents' circular identify the proprietor in Room 311 as **WALTER VALE**. The relief porter belongs to 309.
5. **Observation glass:** reverse `YEK EHT NRUT TON OD` to **DO NOT TURN THE KEY**. Spaces and punctuation are ignored.
6. **Chronology:** **doors opened → clocks stopped → first guest returned**, corresponding to 03, 04, and 05 October 1987.
7. **Final discharge:** present the **original register**, **leave the key untouched**, and take the **service stair**. This closes the accounts and reveals the true ending, **For the first time, morning**.
8. Accepting the uniform instead records **The new night porter**. You can reject that appointment and retry the final discharge without losing progress.
